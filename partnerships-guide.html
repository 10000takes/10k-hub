Partnerships Tab Guide

Use this workspace to manage prospects, partnership leads, current and past clients, communications, revenue, and monthly deliverables.

Private CRM: records are stored in the Partnerships Worker database. Use your individual Jack or Cody access token, and connect only on a private device. Never place access tokens in repository files, spreadsheets, or email. The audit log attributes changes to the signed-in user.

Connect

  1. Enter the Partnerships Worker URL and your named-user access token, then choose Connect. Confirm the displayed name and role.
  2. The token can be remembered in the current browser profile. Choose Forget Access to remove it from that browser.
  3. The site PIN unlocks the page; the Worker token authenticates CRM requests. Keep both private.

Prospects

The Prospects view contains the lead pipeline and researched brands. Use Add Lead for an opportunity you intend to pursue, or Add Researched Brand to save a sourced company for later qualification. Do not treat an unverified research prospect as contacted or as an active opportunity.

Move leads through New → Contacted → Proposal Sent → Closed using the stage selector or drag-and-drop. Set the separate Closed Outcome to Won or Lost. Search, filter, and sort the pipeline; CSV import is available from Import CSV. Add a next follow-up date to active leads so they appear in priorities when due.

Open a record with Edit to update its details, log calls or meetings, and review its Activity Timeline and Communications. A researched prospect can be promoted to a lead when qualified; promotion preserves its company details, contacts, research notes, and communication history.

Research and AI assistance

Research & Discovered Prospects searches public news coverage. Open the source and verify relevance before saving a company. Search results do not verify budgets, decision-makers, contact details, or buying intent. AI lead scoring, sales advice, role suggestions, and proposal language are optional drafts based on supplied CRM information. Verify facts, use approved pricing, and review generated content before sharing.

Today’s Priorities highlights leads with overdue or missing next actions and clients with upcoming contract dates. Partnership Recommendations surfaces follow-up, proposal, and renewal reminders. These are prompts for review, not automatic actions.

Communication history and Gmail

Open a lead or client to see logged activities and the Communications timeline, including matched Gmail metadata and Resend records. The timeline shows last contacted, last reply, and next follow-up when available. Gmail is authoritative for Gmail sent, received, and draft status; Resend outreach remains a separate source. Matching and message IDs prevent the same communication from being counted twice.

The Gmail Synchronization section shows connection status and offers Sync Now, Reconcile History Again, and Disconnect Gmail. Sync reads message metadata only: message bodies and attachments are not read or stored. It retains messages matched to a CRM contact, a known company website, or an already linked thread. A disconnect removes the Worker’s Gmail authorization; existing CRM history is retained.

Gmail sync does not send messages. Any outreach draft must be reviewed by a person, and sending always requires explicit human approval through the authorized email workflow.

Current Clients

The Current Clients view contains current client records and account totals. Expand Client Records to review or edit a client, or choose Add Client to add one. Compensation models include Monthly Retainer, Per Deliverable, and Trade. Cash MRR excludes Trade partnerships; contract value for Per Deliverable clients is shown only when the agreed quantity is recorded.

A valid Contract End Date controls the client list automatically: dates before today place a client in Past Clients; editing that date to a future date returns the client to Current Clients. If there is no contract end date, Contract Status determines the list.

Cash Received (Logged)

Use this form to record cash actually received for a client and month. The chart shows logged receipts, not contracted revenue or MRR. A blank month means no receipt was entered; it does not mean $0 was confirmed.

Monthly Deliverables Tracker

Add a monthly commitment for each recurring deliverable, choose a month, and mark Completed when the full commitment is finished. Unchecking resets that item to zero for the selected month. The green bar and percentage beside the month filter summarize completed units across all commitments for that month. Cards show completed and committed counts without individual percentages. The tracker keeps month-by-month completion history; use the Campaign Tracker to record post links and performance.

Past Clients

The Past Clients view keeps inactive or expired client records and their history available. Update a valid contract end date to a future date to move a client back to Current Clients. For a client without an end date, update Contract Status. Historical revenue entries remain separate from current recurring revenue.

Good data practices

  • Use verified names, titles, emails, source links, and dates. Leave unknown information blank instead of guessing.
  • Keep research notes and follow-up dates on the related prospect or lead. Log meaningful calls, meetings, and other activity.
  • Review AI-generated facts, recommendations, and pricing before using them.
  • Use a permitted file-sharing link for contract documents; the URL field stores a link and does not upload or secure the document.
  • Review recipients and message content before every outreach send. The CRM does not autonomously send email.
