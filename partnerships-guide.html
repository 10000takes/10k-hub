Partnerships Tab Guide

A practical guide for Cody and anyone using the 10K Takes client, contract, prospect, revenue, and deliverables workspace.

What this workspace is: a private CRM backed by the 10K Takes Partnerships Worker and D1 database. The page has separate Prospects, Current Clients, and Past Clients tabs. Jack and Cody use individual access tokens; changes are attributed to the signed-in user in the audit log. Outbound email always requires a human to review and confirm each message. Existing clients, suppressed recipients, and recent duplicate sends are blocked.

Connect to the Workspace

  1. When the connection panel is shown, enter the Partnerships Worker URL and your individual Jack or Cody access token, then choose Connect. The dashboard displays the authenticated name and role.
  2. After a successful connection, the token is remembered in that browser profile and the CRM reconnects automatically on later visits. It is not shared across browsers or devices. Choose Forget Access in the connected workspace to remove it from that browser.
  3. Only remember the token on a private device. Anyone who can use that browser profile can use its CRM access. The dashboard PIN unlocks the site; your named Worker token authenticates CRM requests. Never put either token in GitHub files, spreadsheets, email, or other public material.

Current Clients

The Current Clients tab contains active client records and the account overview. Records marked Active contribute to the Active Clients and monthly revenue totals.

The overview summarizes:

  • Monthly revenue: sum of monthly revenue on active client records.
  • Active clients: count of records marked Active.
  • Total contracted value: sum of contract values entered on client records.
  • New revenue this month: monthly revenue on client records created this calendar month. This is not a payment ledger.
  • Proposals: leads in the Proposal Sent stage.
  • Contracts expiring: current clients with a contract end date within the next 90 days.

The Current Clients tab focuses on active client records, deliverables, and account-level totals.

Cash Received (Logged)

Use the form to log revenue actually received for a client and month. Entries are retained in the private Worker database and shown in a rolling monthly chart. A dash means no receipt has been entered for that month; it does not mean revenue was confirmed to be $0. The chart does not read a client's monthly revenue or contract value. Enter corrections as separate, clearly described entries.

Monthly Deliverables Tracker

Add one commitment for each recurring client deliverable (for example, four short videos each month). Choose a month and check Completed when the full commitment is complete; unchecking it resets that month to zero completed units. The tracker shows completion against the commitment and keeps month-by-month history. Record published URLs on the other screen used for post tracking. Define commitments once per client; changing the plan should be reflected in a new or revised definition before tracking subsequent months.

Expand Client Records to view the list or choose Add Client to enter a current sponsor. Use Edit to update its record. The wider form displays three fields per row on desktop and stores company/contact details, contract dates, Monthly Recurring Revenue, payment frequency, contract document URL, and internal notes. Total Contract Value is calculated automatically as Monthly Recurring Revenue multiplied by the inclusive number of contract months between the start and expiration dates. Payment frequency options are Monthly, Quarterly, and Annually.

Contract Status determines whether the record appears under Current Clients or Past Clients. Only Active clients are included in active client and monthly revenue totals.

The Contract Document URL field stores a link only; it does not upload or secure the document. Use a file-sharing link with appropriate permissions. Avoid putting confidential documents in a public repository.

Past Clients

The Past Clients tab contains client records with Contract Status set to Inactive. Edit a record and change its status to move it between the current and past client lists. Past records remain available for reference and future re-engagement.

Historical partnership records imported from the Partnership History workbook are marked inactive. Repeated partner names were consolidated, with the source rows and amounts retained in the record and activity history. The imported monthly amounts are historical amounts collected per month; they are not current recurring revenue. The workbook did not provide most contract end dates. The previously unnamed $1,999.99 row was identified as Chill Boys and is now included in its history.

Prospects

The Prospects tab combines lead management, public-source discovery, AI sales advice, and prospecting controls. Choose Add Lead to create a company you may pursue. The stage dropdown on a card or drag-and-drop moves a lead through these four stages:

New → Contacted → Proposal Sent → Closed. New leads start in New. Set the separate Closed Outcome to Won or Lost when closing a lead. Existing Qualified leads are moved to New, and existing Proposal leads are moved to Proposal Sent. Older labels are mapped into these four stages.

The Add Lead form captures company details, source, campaign ideas, contact information, and follow-up dates. Industry is selected from a standard list shared by client, lead, and prospect forms. The form no longer asks for priority, fit score, value estimates, closing probability, or a contact-source URL. Changing a stage adds an entry to the lead’s Activity Timeline. Open a card with Edit to add notes or log a call, meeting, email, proposal, or other activity.

The lead search, stage/industry/source filters, and sort menu sit above the pipeline. CSV import expects a header row; useful column names include company_name, industry, stage, website, lead_source, priority, estimated_monthly_value, estimated_total_value, probability, last_contact, and next_follow_up. Duplicate company names in the Leads category are skipped.

Today’s Priorities flags open leads that need a next action and client contracts ending soon. Partnership Recommendations shows cross-CRM follow-up, proposal, and renewal reminders. These sections are available only in Prospects. Pipeline totals and stage counts are shown on the lead board.

Brand Discovery and Agencies

Expand Research & Discovered Prospects, enter a query, and choose Search News to search Google News RSS for public coverage. Open the cited article, verify the company and Minnesota/sports relevance, and use Save as Prospect to keep a sourced research lead. The saved item is explicitly marked unverified. Search does not crawl social media, confirm sponsorship budgets, or prove buying intent. Research decision-maker roles using company-published pages and record source links; do not infer private contact information.

Weekly discovery, qualification, and outreach-draft targets are planning ranges, not quotas; the monthly cost limit is configurable up to $50.

AI Lead Scoring, Advice, and Proposals

In Prospects, select a lead and choose Score Lead, Sales Advice, Proposal / Pricing Draft, or Research Relevant Decision-maker Roles, then choose Generate Draft. The assistant receives selected CRM fields and any pricing assumptions entered in the form. It can suggest roles to research, but it does not verify a person's identity or contact details. Review evidence and assumptions; never rely on generated budgets, audience figures, contacts, or deal terms without verification. Enter only approved rates and review proposal language before sharing.

AI use is optional and requires the Cloudflare Workers AI binding to be configured for this Worker. Until it is enabled, the dashboard shows an explanatory unavailable message. AI processing may incur usage charges; check the provider’s current pricing before enabling it.

Sales Outreach Email

The Partnerships dashboard does not currently create Gmail drafts or send email. Use verified CRM contact details and AI advice as research inputs, then prepare, review, and send outreach manually in Gmail. AI-generated Gmail drafts and inbox synchronization are not implemented.

Data Quality and Boundaries

  • News discovery finds public articles only; it does not automatically identify verified decision-makers, private emails, sponsorship budgets, or buying intent.
  • AI scoring and proposal/pricing suggestions are drafts, not approved prices or a guarantee of deal likelihood. The model only has the CRM facts provided in its request.
  • The historical revenue chart reflects manually entered revenue receipts. It is only complete if all receipts are entered and reconciled.
  • The monthly deliverables tracker is separate from the deliverables notes field. Its checkbox marks the full monthly commitment complete; publication links are managed on the post-tracking screen.
  • Review each message and recipient in Gmail before sending. AI advice is not an approved email draft.
  • Worker access is attributed to Jack or Cody and changes are written to the security log. The current roles identify users for accountability; granular restrictions between these two roles are not yet implemented.
Data entry tip: use real figures when known, leave estimates blank when uncertain, set a next follow-up date for active leads, and include a source when recording researched prospects. Never treat an estimate as confirmed budget or buying intent. Unlabeled historical amounts should remain unassigned until their partner is verified.

Owner Setup Notes

The Worker must accept the same four pipeline stages shown in the dashboard. When deploying a new version, deploy the Sales Worker and publish the GitHub Pages dashboard from the same code revision. Jack and Cody's individual tokens belong only in Cloudflare Worker secrets as SALES_JACK_TOKEN and SALES_CODY_TOKEN; never store them in GitHub or this repository.

Optional AI setup requires the Worker AI binding named AI. Keep all credentials in Worker secrets, not page files or repository variables.
